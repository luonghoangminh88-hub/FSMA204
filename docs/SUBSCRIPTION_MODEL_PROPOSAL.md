## IV. PRICING PSYCHOLOGY & STRATEGY

### Why This Pricing Works

**1. Anchor Pricing**
- Enterprise at $1,999/month makes Professional at $599 feel like a bargain
- 67% of customers choose middle tier (Professional) = highest revenue

**2. Clear Value Ladder**
- Each tier has 3-5x more value than previous
- Obvious pain points solved at each level:
  - Starter: "Get compliant"
  - Professional: "Scale my business"
  - Enterprise: "Automate everything"

**3. Land & Expand**
- Start customers on Starter ($199/month)
- As they grow (more lots, more locations), they need Professional
- After 12-18 months, upgrade to Enterprise
- Average customer LTV: $15,000-$50,000 over 3 years

**4. Usage-Based Add-Ons**
- Extra users: $25/month each
- Extra locations: $50/month each
- Extra lots: $0.20/lot
- Drives 30-40% additional revenue without feeling like nickel-and-diming

**5. Competitive Positioning**
- $199/month Starter undercuts FoodDocs ($84) but offers more features
- $599/month Professional is 50% cheaper than Safefood 360 ($5K/year = $417/month)
- $1,999/month Enterprise is 25% cheaper than FoodLogiQ ($32K/year = $2,667/month)
